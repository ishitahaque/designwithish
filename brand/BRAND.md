# Design with Ish — brand spec

Direction: **Quiet material, warmed.** Concrete, oak, linen, honey-toned wood. Rooms that calm you down, with warmth in the details. Brutalist bones softened by natural materials and late light.

Locked 2026-11-09.

## Color

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | Oat | `#F0E9DE` | Page background, light surfaces |
| Ground 2 | Concrete | `#BFB6A8` | Secondary surfaces, image placeholders, dividers at 100% |
| Text | Charcoal | `#2B2B2B` | All body and headline text, dark surfaces |
| Accent | Moss | `#5E6B4F` | Links, primary mark square, small emphasis. Passes AA on Oat for text. |
| Warm accent | Honey | `#C9933F` | Second mark square, one underline per page, one block in a composition. Never for text on light grounds (fails contrast). |
| Rule | Linen | `#D4CBBE` | Hairlines, card borders |
| Muted text | Stone | `#6F685E` | Captions, secondary text. Passes AA on Oat. |

Rules of thumb: Oat and Charcoal carry 90% of any layout. Moss is the working accent. Honey appears once or twice per screen, never more — it's a dash, not a theme. No gradients.

## Type

- **Headlines:** Archivo, weight 500, tracking −0.03em, line-height 1.05. Sentence case. Google Fonts.
- **Body:** Lora, weight 400, line-height 1.6, max 60–65 characters per line. Italic for occasional emphasis.
- **UI labels, buttons, captions:** Archivo 400/500, 13–15px.
- **Wordmark:** Archivo 500, all lowercase: `design with ish`.

Scale (desktop → mobile): display 64→40, h1 48→32, h2 32→26, h3 22→20, body 17→16, small 14→13.

Google Fonts link:
```
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Archivo:wght@400;500;600&family=Lora:ital,wght@0,400;1,400&display=swap
```

## Space and shape

- Base unit 8px. Section padding 96px desktop / 56px mobile. Gutters 24px.
- Corners: 0px on blocks and images, 2px on form fields. Buttons are square-cornered, outlined (1.5px Charcoal) or filled Charcoal with Oat text.
- Hairlines 1px Linen. Dividers between sections, not around every card.
- Photography: natural light, no filters, show material texture up close. Crop to 4:5 or 3:2. Let images breathe with Oat around them.

## Mark

Two 14px squares, Moss then Honey, 4px apart, sitting above or left of the lowercase wordmark. Files in `brand/logo/`.

## Voice

Calm, short sentences. Describe how a room feels at a time of day, not just how it looks. Name materials: oak, linen, plaster, travertine. Confident without lecturing. Lowercase energy — never shouting, never selling.

Say: "settled," "warm," "quiet," "honest materials," "what matters."
Avoid: "elevate," "luxury," "stunning," "transform," exclamation marks, superlatives.

Sample headline: *Fewer things, chosen slowly.*
Sample hero: *Rooms that let you breathe.*
Sample intro: *Spaces for boutique businesses and homes that want to feel settled and warm. I take rooms down to what matters, then bring in material, wood and light.*
