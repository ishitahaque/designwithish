Work / Vacation home

Modern ranch house in Brasada

The high desert, without the cowboy costume.

The challenge

A house at Brasada Ranch should feel like Brasada Ranch. The risk is tipping into a theme. The owners also rent it to guests, so every piece had to stand up to a full house, week after week.

Where
Brasada Ranch, Oregon
When
2025
Scope
Furnishing and lighting
Rooms
Common areas, especially living and dining

The decisions

Borrow the ranch's colors and textures, and keep the lines modern. The living and dining rooms carry most of it, because that is where a house full of guests actually gathers.

The materials

Caramel leather, live-edge wood, brass, and olive, the palette of the landscape outside the windows.

The outcome

Brasada, brought indoors.

The house reads as the ranch without leaning western, and it is set up for the way it is used: as a place people arrive at, drop their bags, and settle in.
