How it works

  1. Call

    Twenty minutes, free. You tell me about the space, how you use it, and what isn't working.

  2. Walkthrough

    I see the room in person or on video, measure, and watch how the light moves through it.

  3. Concept

    You get a layout, a material palette, and a sourced list. We revise together until it feels like yours.

  4. Install

    Orders are placed, deliveries tracked, and on install day everything gets placed and styled.
