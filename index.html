How a project runs

  1. Call

    A 30-minute conversation about what you need, what's already in place, and when you need it. Free, no deck.

  2. Proposal

    A one-page scope with a fixed price, a timeline, and exactly what you'll receive. You sign, we start.

  3. Design

    Weekly check-ins with real work on the table each time. Two rounds of revisions are included at every stage.

  4. Handoff

    Final files, source files, and a walkthrough. For websites, the site goes live on your domain before I send the last invoice.

About

I've spent the last decade designing for companies at very different scales, and I kept noticing the same thing: the most useful work happens when the person designing is also in the room for the decisions. So I went independent to keep that closeness.

I work with a small number of clients at a time, which means you get my attention rather than a junior's. I'm based in Portland, Oregon, and work with teams anywhere.
