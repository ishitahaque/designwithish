How I think about a room

Balance first, then one brave choice.

Balance

Many materials, edited hard. A room should hold contrast without tipping into noise.

Boldness

Where I grew up, textile, color, and pattern were how a home was made. Every room gets one thing that takes a risk.

Function

A room has to work on an ordinary Tuesday: for guests, for clients, for the way you move through it.
