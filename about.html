I grew up in a narrow row house in Brooklyn, where my mother fit Bangladesh into every room.

Where it started

Textile, color, and pattern were simply how a home was made. Making a small space hold a lot of life was the first design lesson I ever got.

What travel added

Italy, more than anywhere, taught me that flamboyance and restraint can share a room. That is still what I look for: balance, boldness, and function, in that order.

How I work

I spent a year as a design consultant with West Elm, working with homeowners and small boutique businesses around Portland, and found that I love the boutique side most. A good room changes how customers feel before anyone says a word.

I notice the small things: where the light lands at four o'clock, which chair people actually choose. And I work with you, not around you. We revise together until the room feels like yours.

The other half

By day I'm an AI product leader. It makes me a better designer in one specific way: I know how to balance a fresh idea with getting it delivered, on a timeline and budget that hold.

I live and work in Portland's Pearl District.
