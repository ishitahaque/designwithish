About

I grew up in a narrow row house in Brooklyn, where my mother fit Bangladesh into every room. Textile, color, and pattern were simply how a home was made, and making a small space hold a lot of life was the first design lesson I ever got.

Travel did the rest. Italy, more than anywhere, taught me that flamboyance and restraint can share a room. That is still what I look for: balance, boldness, and function, in that order.

I spent a year as a design consultant with West Elm, working with homeowners and small boutique businesses around Portland, and found that I love the boutique side most. A good room changes how customers feel before anyone says a word.

By day I'm an AI product leader, so I know how to balance innovation with delivery. Your project gets both: a fresh idea, and a timeline and budget that hold. I live and work in Portland's Pearl District.
