Work / Med spa

Komal Aesthetics & Wellness

An office suite in Hillsboro, turned into a med spa where the calm starts before the appointment does.

Where
Hillsboro, Oregon
Client
Komal Aesthetics & Wellness
When
2025
Scope
Furnishing, layout, color, lighting
Style
Wabi-sabi

The challenge

The space began as a plain office suite, and it felt like one: cold. A med spa asks for the opposite. People should feel looked after from the moment they sit down.

The decisions

I rebuilt it around warmth, in the wabi-sabi spirit: quiet, imperfect, and natural. The layout was softened, the color taken down to earthen tones, and the lighting lowered so it glows instead of glaring.

The materials

Soft things against textured ones. Boucle and velvet to sink into, walls with a hand-worked finish, fluted wood, stone, and brass.

The outcome

From cold to warm and inviting.

That is the part I'm proudest of. The warmth carries from the waiting area through to the treatment rooms.
