Med spa, Hillsboro, Oregon, 2025

Komal Aesthetics & Wellness

A plain office suite became a wabi-sabi med spa. The room started out cold, all hard light and blank walls. We rebuilt it around warmth: a softer layout, earthen color, low lighting, and furniture you want to sink into before an appointment.

Modern ranch house in Brasada

My own loft, Pearl District, Portland, 2026

The Marshall-Wells loft

I live in a 1910 hardware warehouse designed by Daniel Burnham, the architect of New York's Flatiron Building. It is one enormous open room. Shoji sliders close off the office when I need walls and disappear when I don't, so the space feels divided and open at once. I call the style tasteful eclectic: many materials, edited hard.

Furnishing, layout, lighting
